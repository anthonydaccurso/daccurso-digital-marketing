### Start With Self-Assessment

Before choosing a career path, ask yourself:
- What are my natural strengths?
- What activities energize me?
- What kind of impact do I want to make?
- What work environment suits me best?

### Explore Different Options

Don't limit yourself too early. Instead:
- Take online courses to test different fields
- Conduct informational interviews with professionals
- Try internships or freelance projects
- Join industry events and networking groups

### Set SMART Goals

When planning your career path, use SMART goals:
- **Specific** - Define exactly what you want to achieve
- **Measurable** - Track your progress with clear metrics
- **Achievable** - Set realistic milestones
- **Relevant** - Align with your values and interests
- **Time-bound** - Set deadlines for accountability

### Embrace the Journey

Your career path doesn't have to be linear. Many successful professionals:
- Change industries multiple times
- Pivot to new roles based on interests
- Combine skills from different backgrounds

**Key Takeaway:** Focus on continuous learning and stay open to new opportunities.
