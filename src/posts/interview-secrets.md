### Prepare Your Stories

Use the **STAR method** to structure your answers:
- **Situation** - Set the context
- **Task** - Explain the challenge
- **Action** - Describe what you did
- **Result** - Share the outcome

### Research the Company

Before any interview:
- Visit their website and social media
- Understand their mission and values
- Review recent news or product launches
- Identify how your skills align with their needs

### Ask Thoughtful Questions

At the end of the interview, ask questions like:
- "What does success look like in this role?"
- "What are the biggest challenges the team is facing?"
- "How would you describe the company culture?"

### Follow Up

Send a thank-you email within 24 hours:
- Express gratitude for the opportunity
- Reiterate your interest in the role
- Reference something specific from the conversation

**Remember:** Confidence, preparation, and authenticity go a long way.
