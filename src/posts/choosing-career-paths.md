### Start With Self-Assessment

Understanding yourself is the foundation of choosing the right career. Consider:
- What are you naturally good at?
- What activities energize you?
- What values are non-negotiable (work-life balance, income, creativity, helping others)?
- What kind of work environment do you thrive in?

### Research Career Options

Once you have a sense of your strengths and preferences, explore careers that align with them. Use resources like:
- Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook
- Informational interviews with professionals in fields that interest you
- Online career assessment tools
- LinkedIn to explore career trajectories

### Consider Growth Potential

Think long-term: Does this career have room for advancement? Are there opportunities to specialize or transition into related fields? Is the industry growing or declining?

### Gain Relevant Experience

Before committing fully, test the waters:
- Internships and co-ops
- Volunteer work
- Side projects
- Online courses and certifications

Real-world experience helps you validate your interest and build practical skills.

### Set Clear Goals

Use the SMART framework to define your career goals:
- **Specific:** What exactly do you want to achieve?
- **Measurable:** How will you track progress?
- **Achievable:** Is this realistic given your circumstances?
- **Relevant:** Does this align with your values and long-term vision?
- **Time-bound:** When do you want to accomplish this?

### Stay Flexible

Career paths are rarely linear. Be open to pivoting as you learn more about yourself and the opportunities available. Your first job doesn't have to be your forever job.

### Seek Mentorship

Connect with mentors who can provide guidance, share their experiences, and help you navigate career decisions. Their insights can be invaluable as you chart your course.
