### Start With the Right Structure

Employers typically scan resumes for key sections. Make sure yours includes:

- **Contact Information** - Name, phone, email, LinkedIn
- **Professional Summary** - 2-3 sentences highlighting your expertise
- **Work Experience** - Listed in reverse chronological order
- **Education** - Degrees, certifications, relevant coursework
- **Skills** - Technical and soft skills relevant to the role

### Use Action Verbs and Quantify Results

Instead of saying "Responsible for managing projects," say:

> "Led 5 cross-functional projects, improving delivery time by 30%"

### Keep It Concise

Your resume should be:
- 1 page for early career professionals
- 2 pages maximum for experienced professionals
- Clean, easy to scan, and results-driven

### Tailor Each Resume

Customize your resume for each application by:
1. Matching keywords from the job description
2. Highlighting relevant experience
3. Emphasizing skills that align with the role

**Tip:** Keep it concise, clean, and results-driven.
